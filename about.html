About the Playlist

I made this playlist from the songs I play the most. Some are songs I listen to in the gym, some are songs I play when I'm studying, and some are older songs I grew up hearing. Each song is stored as an object in a JSON array, and every object has the same 8 fields.

    Every song object has an id, title, artist, genre, length, year, rating out of 5 and whether it's one of my favourites. The length is stored as text like "3:45", and the website splits it up to work out the total time of the playlist. You can add, delete and search songs on the Playlist page.
